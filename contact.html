CONTACT & FEEDBACK

A little help, when you need it.

Talk to us on GitHub.

EpuBloom is maintained by ficbase. Our public contact channel is GitHub Issues. Search existing reports, or sign in to GitHub and open a new issue.

A useful report includes…

  • The conversion direction: TXT to EPUB, or EPUB to TXT.
  • Your browser version, device operating system, and approximate file size.
  • The error message, or what you expected and what actually happened.
  • A short example you may share publicly, and the original encoding if known.

Issues are public. Please use a small example you wrote yourself, rather than private drafts, complete copyrighted books, account details, or screenshots with personal information.

Questions, privacy, or a better idea?

Use the same channel for questions about this site, its privacy notice, or improvements you’d like to see. Maintainers can follow up in the issue. We don’t promise a fixed response time.
